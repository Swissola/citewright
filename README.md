# citewright

**Adoption with receipts.**

CITE: **C**laims, **I**mpact, **T**rust, **E**vidence.

An evidence-graded, fully cited review of how to drive AI adoption, including
agentic quality assurance, in software organisations. It compares two starting
points: an established organisation and a start-up about two years old. Every
figure is attributed to a source and graded for strength, and anything that is
the author's judgement is labelled as such.

*Evidence current to 10 October 2026. Edition: v1.1.3.*

## Start here

- **Short on time:** read the [executive brief](executive-brief.md), two pages.
- **Planning a rollout:** Sections 4 to 8 of the report, the [readiness check](readiness-check.md) to choose a first move, then the [templates](templates.md).
- **Sceptical of a number:** go straight to the evidence register.

## How to read the evidence

| Grade | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A | Controlled study with a disclosed method |
| B | Large survey or telemetry, often vendor produced, mostly correlational |
| C | Case study, conference talk, practitioner or legal commentary |

Statements tagged *Judgement* are the author's synthesis, not findings.
Citation numbers such as [7] refer to `references.md`.

## Limitations

Much of the quantitative evidence comes from vendors and is correlational.
Some sources were read only as summaries or trade-press coverage. The report's
limitations section lists these in full. Please read it before relying on any
single figure.

## Corrections
